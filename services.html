Case Study

Kelliworks
Financial Services

A Florida-based financial services firm generating consistent revenue — but bleeding hours every week to manual onboarding, fragmented intake processes, and a marketing workflow with no single owner. The CEO was personally involved in tasks that had no business requiring their attention.

The Problems

  • ✕

    New hires took weeks to onboard. Every one required 1–2 hours of the CEO's time, repeatedly.

  • ✕

    Client intake was a manual time sink. Contracts, CRM updates, and calendar booking took an hour of staff time per client — every time.

  • ✕

    Marketing approvals were scattered across three platforms with no clear owner and no unified workflow.

  • ✕

    Potential clients needed a full sales engagement before receiving any financial insight — delaying the relationship from the start.
